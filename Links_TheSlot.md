# The Slot

## What is it?

### Videos

1. [US GOLF TV: Todd Kolb | How to Get Your Golf Swing In the Slot - Golf Tips (video) | 2020](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2UimYRrPdBk)
   - He defines *the slot* and *over the top*
   - Be in the slot on the takeaway so you can be in the slot on the downswing

1. [Scratch Golf Academy: Adam Bazalgette | Golf Swing Drills - Drop Your Arms and [GET IN THE SLOT!!] (video) | 2020](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v7-PYDjpGF0)
   - Drills/feels near the end

1. [McLean Golf | Golf Tips Jim McLean Slot Swing (video) | 2010](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8E6Mu6Y556s)
   - Jim McLean, author of the book *The Slot Swing*
   - Three swing shapes to get into the slot


### Articles

1. [Today's Golfer: Chris Ryan | The key move all good golfers make (and you can practise it at home) | 2021](https://www.todays-golfer.com/features/instruction-features/2020/march/golf-swing-slot/)
   - Very detailed
   - The delivery position | Halfway through the downswing [about position P6]
   - The body – how the body should work in the golf swing |
     The arms – what the arms should do during the golf swing |
     The club – how the golf club works in harmony with your rotation

1. [SportsRec | How to Get the Slot in Your Golf Swing | 2023](https://www.sportsrec.com/13733569/slot-golf-swing-20409html/)
   - It's a position halfway into your downswing from where you can hit the ball on a slightly in-to-out path.
   - The lower part of your swing plane, starting when your right elbow drops down close to your right side.

1. [My Golf Instructor: Maria Palozola | How to get back to the slot? | c.2014](https://www.mygolfinstructor.com/instruction/diagnosing-problems/tips/slot/459/)
   - Good players swing to the top, drop the club on plane and then turn to the target.
   - [Swing Plane: The Heart of the Swing](https://www.mygolfinstructor.com/instruction/diagnosing-problems/swing-plane/)
