1. [fitzygolfpro: Brian Fitzgerald | Lag Putting Fundamentals Every Golfer Needs (video) | 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QOVk3g493us)
   - 3 keys: Use a bigger backswing | Constant tempo e.g. 52 bpm (48-56) metronome rate | Don't look up too soon

1. [Bettinardi Golf | Lag Putting with Sir Nick Faldo (video) | 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XGrOCzw5-gs)
   - Distance drill: Pay attention to the distance your hand travels when you putt | Measure the resulting putting distance (in paces) |
     Constant tempo | E.g. A 2 inch backswing (and 2 inch follow through) with your hand results in the ball rolling 5 paces
   - Practice a single putt (not several putts) at various distances

1. [Golf Secrets | HOW TO MASTER PUTTING SPEED (Phil won the 2006 MASTERS with this putting drill) (video) | 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oltGp7WqC8w)
   - Phil Mickelson explains and demonstrates the 40, 50, 60 foot putting drill
   - Two variables: Backstroke length | The amount of energy used going forward | Eliminate one variable by keeping the second variable constant
   - Practice on uphill and downhill sloping greens
